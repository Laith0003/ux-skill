# print-layout

- **Proof print work on paper at actual size.** Size, tactility and color are only judged correctly in print, so print several times during the process; on-screen proofs are not valid for spot-color matching. Confidence: medium. Sources: B10 p.154; B76 p.93, 108-110; B64 p.187
- **Prepare files at print resolution and in the right color space.** Supply raster images at 100 percent or more of final size at about 300 ppi, and use CMYK or spot colors for print and RGB for screen. Confidence: low. Sources: B05 p.181, 246
- **Proof spot colors as ink drawdowns on the real stock.** The same ink looks different on coated, uncoated and matte paper, and printed swatch books fade, so replace them about every two years. Confidence: low. Sources: B76 p.93, 108-112
- **Keep inner margins wide enough to survive the binding.** Text near the spine is lost, outer margins need room for thumbs, and small text should never run across a central gutter. Confidence: medium. Sources: B10 p.50, 56, 78; B92 p.96
- **Progress margins from spine to foot in facing pages.** Order the margins from narrowest at the spine through the head and fore-edge to widest at the foot; a fore-edge roughly double the spine suits thin booklets, and a top margin between one ninth and one twelfth of the page makes a sound start. Confidence: low. Sources: B92 p.96-97; B10 p.25-31
- **Use a classical canon when the book form calls for it.** On a 2:3 page, inner, top, outer and bottom margins set in the ratio 2:3:4:6 leave the inner margin at half the outer and the top at half the bottom. Confidence: low. Sources: B10 p.24-31; B92 p.94
- **Leave margins that fit both A4 and US Letter.** Documents that cross regions must print on both 210 x 297 mm and 8.5 x 11 in without clipping. Confidence: low. Sources: B08 p.53; B10 p.37-41
- **Ship a print stylesheet that strips page chrome.** Remove navigation and other clutter when a web page is printed. Confidence: low. Sources: B59 p.16
- **Keep a presentation substrate neutral and uniform.** Mounts, cases and boards should recede behind the work, in one standard size. Confidence: low. Sources: B84 p.15, 79
- **Move long explanatory text off image spreads.** A separate index keyed to page numbers keeps spreads clean while still giving context. Confidence: low. Sources: B84 p.82
- **Choose a format that shows the work at a truthful scale.** Large-scale work needs a format big enough to read at scale, within what is practical to carry and mail. Confidence: low. Sources: B84 p.82, 121, 161

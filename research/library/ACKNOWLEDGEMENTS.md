# Acknowledgements

ux-skill learned from these 96 books. Their lessons were read, weighed against each other and against the current standards, merged by topic and rewritten in our own words. Nothing here quotes them, and no lesson is credited to a single person, because each one is a blend. Any mistake in the blend is ours.

Thank you to every author below for writing down what you learned so the rest of us could build on it. If something here helped you, buy their books.

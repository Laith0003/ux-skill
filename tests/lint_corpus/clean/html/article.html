Why we removed the delivery countdown

By the routing team, 14 August 2026

Customers told us the ticking clock made them anxious. When a truck ran five minutes late, the countdown went negative and support calls doubled.

I watched the timer instead of doing my job.

An arrival window of thirty minutes, updated from live GPS, answered the real question: should I wait by the door? Calls about late deliveries dropped by a third in the first month.
